### 6a. Browser work goes through `bp`
Any browser exploration or testing in this loop, by you or an implementer, uses `bp`, which lives in this skill's `bp/` (rules in `bp/README.md`).
Never the user's real Chrome or ad hoc Playwright scripts.
- **Exploring** (what does the panel show, did the fill work, record a flow): `bp launch --ext <dir>`, `bp list`, `bp hosts`, `bp snap`, `bp clicktext`, `bp form`, `bp record`.
  Screenshots land in `shots/` with an `index.md` line each.
- **Driving toward a goal with the model**: `bp goal <url> "<goal> … stop before Submit"`.
  Same jev policy and Chromium as verify's e2e.
- **Verify e2e for a web app**: nothing to configure.
  The stage launches and ends its own `bp` Chromium (section 6).
- **Verify e2e for an extension**: the stage's browser has no extension, so launch the extension's Chromium in `e2e_setup` and point jev at it with `e2e_env`; a `BU_CDP_URL` there replaces the stage's browser, and the stage still ends the browser on that CDP port:
  ```json
  "e2e_setup": "BP_PORT=9333 bp launch about:blank --ext /path/to/extension/dist --profile /tmp/bp-profile-verify",
  "e2e_setup_wait": 4,
  "e2e_env": {"BU_CDP_URL": "http://127.0.0.1:9333", "BU_NAME": "bp9333"},
  "e2e": [{"name": "greenhouse fill", "url": "https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/...", "goal": "Open the Jobscan panel, click Autofill, wait for the checklist, stop before Submit.",
           "assert": "bp form greenhouse.io | grep -q 'filled [1-9]'"}]
  ```
  A scripted check with no model is a `driver` case: `{"name": "panel renders", "driver": "bp hosts greenhouse.io --min 50 | grep -q Autofill", "env": {"BP_PORT": "9333"}}`.
- One port per extension build.
  Never let a goal or driver click a real employer Submit; assert on `bp form` output and a screenshot.
